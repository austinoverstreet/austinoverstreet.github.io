# Well, here we go again.

I know for a fact that i’ve tried many times before to write/use a blog. Most of the time I do it for a few days then end up completely forgetting about it for months. Then I end up deleting everything from it. Either continuing with life or creating another one. I’m going to promise myself this time will be different.
That’s a lie.
I know deep down that nothing is going to be different about this blog. The biggest change now is that i’m creating something myself instead of using blogger or a different site. No, i’m working within my obisidan vault to create a site. Then, I can just post from there. It seems like a nice idea, gives me the ability to post from anywhere, even to write from anywhere and save it. I doubt it’s going to go great. If I’m being honest with myself, I’ll forget about all of this in a few days.
But, what if?
See, I live my life by that simple question. That’s the only question you ever need to ask yourself when doubting your capibilities. What if? What if I can? What if I do? I’m asking myself that question now. If I end up sticking with this, it could one day help my career as a write. It also is a great outlet for me to write and get my thoughts out of my head. Which is something that I need. When my mind is clear, everything else in life is that much more clear for me. Writing helps that process as a whole.
I’ve beening using claude to help me get everything ready and built. I didn’t think it would be as fun or interesting as it is. Hard work? Sure. At that same time, it’s extremely rewarding.
I have so much to talk about and very little time before I need to go to bed. I have a full day of work tomorrow that I’d like to have rested for. I also know that writing is important to me so I should finish this up before going to bed.
Since this is truly the first post I am making, it’s time for updates.

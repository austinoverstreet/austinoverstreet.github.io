# Who says?

In life, we are given a set of rules and told to follow them. Never learning where those rules came from, only knowing that they must be abided by. Now, that is no inherently a good nor bad thing. At-least, I don’t think that. I believe in most cases, these unwritten rules that we follow are for the good of society. Which as implied, is good.

Still, Who says?

John Mayer, was never an artist that I wanted to run to and listen. In fact, I didn’t think highly of him until college. That opinion was something formed of ignorance. I’d never listened to his music, except for what was played on the radio. Furthermore, when I was growing up, being a guy and liking John Mayer wasn’t exactly the cool thing.

While in college that changed.

I came home from class and found my roommate sitting in our living room listening to John Mayer. I think I said "turn this shit off" as I walked in. To which he protested and ultimately won. It wasn’t a night and day switch where I suddenly became a fan of John. This was my sophomore year and I wouldn’t become a fan until the following year. Even then, I didn’t begin to like John because of his music. I began to like him because of the Grateful Dead. Prior to college, sure I knew a handful of Dead songs, but they weren’t one of my go-to bands. In fact, I didn’t care for them all that much. I was more of a Phish guy. I started listening to them in high school after watching a show live on the couch with my uncle.

Phish hooked me. Pun intended.

Even when I got to college, it wasn’t something that I advertised. I may have had Ghost blaring in my headphone but no one knew that. To me, it was a secret. Outwardly people saw me as someone who mainly listened to rap, a little classic rock, and country. Inward, I was jamming. I don’t know when or how I started getting into the Dead. Just happened one day. Which is funny enough of itself since I have a Grateful Dead shirt from the 90s that I’ve been wearing since I was in middle school. I got it from my mom, who was never a big deadhead but had a love for their shirts. She gave me a few, but this one specific shirt is something I wear often.

Once I got into the dead, I quickly became a fan of John.

See, I had some misconception about him and his music. Not because it was bad but because I’d never given it a chance. I’d never seen it or him for what he was. He may have been labeled many things, but he didn’t choose those. Often we don’t choose the labels that identify us. Once I was able to look past that. Once I found common ground in the dead. I saw him for the musician that he is, and boy he is a hell of a musician.

Again, who says?

We may not choose the labels that define us, but we are able to choose how we respond to the world around us. We’re able to break the mold and look past outward appearances and see the skeleton beneath. That doesn’t mean that there still aren’t people who suck. It doesn’t mean that the labels are bad. Simply put, it means we have to decide on our own how we feel about things.

We may not have written these societal rules.

We didn’t choose the life we were born into.

But, we are able to wake up every day and make choices. If those are positive or negative, it’s entirely up to us. How we respond to others, is up to us. How we chose to see the world, is up to us. We all are going to make decision based on labels or other stupid shit. That’s part of the human experience.

Next time, ask yourself, who says?

Then remember, it’s you.

## Writing

Things are going okay with my novels right now. I finally have my new writing platform all set up, and I’m very excited to dive into it and get my hands dirty. I have quite a bit of work to do with all my different series. So, I’m sorry if this is a very boring update. At least it won't be a brief one.

#### Echoes of Rapture

Ezra’s tale is going very. Very well. I would say that I’m roughly 80-85% of the way done with it. For a while I was posting it on Royal Road. Sadly, I have stopped that. It was going well, but the time commitment was a little extreme with having a full-time job. I know, I know. If you want to make it as a writer than you have to write. Look, you don’t have to tell me that. But, sitting down to write 4 to 5 thousand words a week and not getting paid for it can be a little taxing. Especially after a long week at work. With that being said, I’m taking time to fall back into love with writing. That way I’m not thinking about it like an unpaid second job, just a hobby for me. Doing that has already brought some of the magic back.

#### Tales of Vicissitude

This book will take me forever to complete. That’s fine by me. I’ve said it before, I’ll say it again, this book will be my masterpiece. I will not rush that.

#### Scott and Jeffrey

Shit, we are on an indefinite hiatus for this bad boy. I really started to hate where the story was going, so I scrapped most of it. Don’t worry. I will rework it and bring it back. Scott and Jeffrey will be the dynamic duo you would expect from an early 2000s stoner movie. This is a story with a lot of heart in it for me. Simply because I would love to be either one of them.

#### Untitled

This is the scary part … I have a lot of untitled/half finished works that are floating on my hard drive. I’m working through them, it’s slow, but I am working. Eventually, I will be able to provide more information on them all. Until that time. Here are a few things of note.

- One of these books is a horror. Following the liminal space idea, but with my own twists.
- I have TWO LitRPGs in the works. Not Dungeons and Dragons, MMO, or even RPG coded. No, one is a BR FPS style but with twists, turns, and a lot of fuckery. The other is just another Soulslike. Very dark. Very grim. Very little hope.
- Finally, I have a dark academia story. Think magic college. Then add monsters, mythical creatures, and Greek life. Sounds fun right? It’s the big trend now. School with magic and dragons! What’s not to love about that. But, none of them have Greek life and most are targeted towards women. So, time to break the mold IO guess. They always say to write what you know. I was in a fraternity in college and my Instagram bio says I’m a Dragon Enthusiast. Guess I’m off to an okay start.

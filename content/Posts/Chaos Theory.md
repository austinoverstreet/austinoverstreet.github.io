Hello there!

For those of you who do not know me, I’m Austin Overstreet, one day to be world reknowned author and story teller. I just want to take a moment to welcome you to my blog/newsletter/public outlet. A place where the only thoughts are my own and you can take a peek behind the curtain. Right now, I doubt anyone will read this.

One day, people will. When that time comes. Thank you.

Without readers, writers are nothing. Just souls bleeding into pages with words and emotions that will others will never experience. Such I do this now because I enjoy it but realistically, I hope one day someone will share in this with me. Feeling what I felt and helped in the same way I have been.

Again, thank you.

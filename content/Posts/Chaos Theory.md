Hello there!

For those of you who do not know me, I’m Austin Overstreet, future best-selling author, story teller, and all around goof ball. I just wanted to take a moment to note only welcome you to my blog/newsletter/outlet but also thank you for taking the time to read the words which I have written. This is nothing more than a place where I can reveal my thoughts and give you a peek behind the curtain. I will share letters, stories, and updates here. Possibly even a few chapter of books that I’m working on!

I wanted to create a space for me and you to connect. Sure, as of today, October 25th of 2026, it’s very likely that no one is going to read this or even see it. Hopefully one day someone will and they will appreciate the thought I put into this.

When that time finally comes along, know that I thank you. Without readers, writers are nothing. Souls bleeding words and emotions into pages, never to be experienced by others. Still for the love of the craft, I write, without audience. Simply for the love of the game with the hope that someday these words will change a life as they have changed mine.

Again, thank you.

Welcome to my own Chaos Theory.

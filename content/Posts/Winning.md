# Winning

## How far we’ve come

It’s so easy to forget how far we’ve come in the grand scheme of life. I myself, succumb to this more than I would like to admit. I’m at the greatest place in life, yet something feels like its missing. I have quite literally everything I could have ever asked for and more. I’ve been thinking about what I could be missing. What could be out there that my soul is still searching for.

I’ve found it.

Along the journey of life, walking down my long path, I lost something within myself. Something that I had to grow and cultivate. Something that made me, me. I wish I could throw a pin in the exact time and place where I lost that piece of me. Unfortunately, I can’t. But, I can look back and see what it was that I lost. From there I can make a plan to get it back.

It’s not an easy plan by any means.

When I was in college, I decided that I was tired of being some typical person. I found out about these great people and wanted to emulate them in my own life. One such person was David Goggins. He is the reason I ran my first ultra-marathon without training. Granted I did run in high school, still, by this point in life I was no longer running like that.

That race changed my life and pulled something from deep within me to the surface.

I started to look at myself like the main character of a story which I was writing. I had crazy dreams that I wanted to go after. Crazy goals to achieve. And, I went after them. I got a job at Lululemon while still in a cast from breaking my hand. I excelled at that job, and it pushed me into something that I never thought I would love as much as I did. It pushed me into motivating people.

By that point in life, I’d done a lot of things, and I was so proud of myself that I wanted to share everything with the world. Which I did. I started watching a lot of motivational videos and even started making my own. I graduated from college during 2020, it was the worst. Not only that, but I didn’t get the traditional experience with graduating from college. Furthermore, I was upset, I’m not afraid to admit that. But, my team at Lululemon made me a video congratulating me. I loved that job, I look back on my time there a lot because I learned so much about myself and leadership while there.

A friend there told me about 75 Hard. I jumped in head first and completed it. It was amazing. I felt like a rock star. I was posting motivational videos to inspire others to do the things I was doing. I lost a lot of weight. I felt amazing.

Then, I left Lululemon to pursue other endeavors.

It’s taken me awhile to get to this point in my life, to say that I’m genuinely happy. But, still I’m missing that piece of me. The motivational leader part had been lost for so long. All those external factors were great during that time. It was the fact that I was trying to do everything to help others that made me truly happy. I haven’t done anything like that in so long. I’ve lost that piece of me, gained weight, and reverted back to who I was before all that.

I don’t mean that in a bad way.

In fact, I see it as a good thing.

Jocko Willink, says “good,” to everything. Because it's a mindset. Instead of looking at something as bad, you think of it as good. Example: You fail a math test. Now, you have two-way to look at it. One: Shit, I failed that, it’s going to ruin my grade. You’re now seeing it as a negative thing, and it’s taking you down a negative path. Two: Good, I failed the test, now I have more time to study and get better. You’re shifting the paradigm of it so that you see it positively. You’re not spiraling down into negativity.

I stopped looking at everything as “good.” Sure I have been still telling people about it, but I haven’t been practicing what I preach. Well, no more. I want to be like Charlie Rocket, a delusional optimist. I want to start my winning streak and quite getting sucked into the negative of life.

I know that it isn’t going to be easy, the best things in life usually aren’t.

So, how am I going to do this? Hopefully that is what you’re asking your self now.

It’s easy. It’s a mindset. I have to wake up and choose to see things from the positive lens. Choose to say “good” to things that I would have previously thought bad. It's not something that’s going to happen overnight because it didn’t before. But, if I try then it will happen.

Winners are simply loser that kept trying.

I have big goals for life.

Big dreams.

Today, we start pushing forward to them.

Today, we start the winning streak.

### Trials by Flame

Published in 2023, this was my first book. It’s not the greatest thing that I’ve written and was published by accident. Still, it’s my writing and I have to be proud of that. It’s simply a collection of poems and short stories. Originally published under Austin Ray, my first and middle names.

> “Take a journey into the mind of a young man. The mind of someone who has been through trials by flame and walked to the other side. This book is a collection of poems and stories to showcase a young writer. While also allowing him to explore himself and his mind.”

[Buy on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Trials-Flame-Austin-Ray/dp/B0CPFVTRRL)

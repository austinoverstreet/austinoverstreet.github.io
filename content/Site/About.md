I have no primary focus in my writing, I will write just about anything. Sure, I have my favorites, mainly dark fantasy or science-fiction but who doesn’t love a good horror story and why wouldn’t I want to write something to motivate children. At the end of the day, I write whatever decides to come out of my mind in that moment. That’s the beauty in writing, tapping into an unknown force and allowing the words to flow onto the page.

## Why Chaos Theory?

Well, simply put, chaos is beautiful and the natural order of things. There is something about it that draws my attention and makes me want to follow it like the sun. It originated when I started using Obsidian to organize my life, I called my vault Chaos Theory. It stuck with me.
